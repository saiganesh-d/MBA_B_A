Programme Overview

The MBA in Business Analytics is a distinguished postgraduate programme designed for ambitious professionals who aspire to lead with precision, insight, and strategic sophistication in an increasingly data-driven world. Seamlessly integrating advanced analytical capability with refined managerial expertise, the programme empowers learners to translate complex data into high-impact business decisions and drive meaningful organisational transformation.

Developed by the esteemed faculty of the Jindal Global Business School (JGBS)—an AACSB-accredited institution recognised among the world's elite business schools—this online MBA offers a comprehensive yet flexible learning experience. With a curriculum that blends conceptual depth, applied analytical training, and real-world problem-solving, the programme equips you with the intellectual confidence and professional credibility required to excel in influential analytics and leadership roles across global industries.
